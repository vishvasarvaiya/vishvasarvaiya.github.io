Delete your Pocket Playland data

App: Pocket Playland: Offline Games · Developer: Sleepysleve

Delete the game data on your phone (instant)

Your scores, coins, levels and settings are stored only on your phone. We do not keep a copy. To delete them:

  1. Open Pocket Playland and tap the menu button (top left).
  2. Tap Reset all scores and coins, then tap it again to confirm.

Or uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's Settings → Apps → Pocket Playland → Storage → Clear storage. Either way, everything is deleted straight away.

Ask us to delete data (by email)

  1. Email sleepysleve@gmail.com with the subject "Pocket Playland data deletion".
  2. Tell us what you would like deleted. If you signed in to Google Play Games, include your Play Games gamer name.
  3. We reply within 30 days and confirm what was deleted.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Game progress and settings: stored only on your device, deleted as described above. We hold nothing on a server.
  • Google Play Games scores (only if you signed in): these are held by Google. You can delete your Play Games profile and its data at any time in the Google Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, or at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.
  • Advertising data collected by Google AdMob (advertising ID, approximate location, ad interactions): held by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Purchase records (Remove ads): kept by Google Play as your proof of purchase, so you can restore it on a new phone. Google keeps these as required for payments and tax.

See also our Privacy Policy.
