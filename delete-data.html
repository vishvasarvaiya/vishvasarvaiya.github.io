Delete your Pocket Playland data

App: Pocket Playland: Offline Games · Developer: Sleepysleve

Delete your online profile (in the app, instant)

If you play online, with friends or send challenges, the app keeps a small online profile on our server (Google Firebase): your player tag, the nickname you chose, your avatar and level, your friends list, friend requests, game invites, challenge scores and 2-Day Cup scores. To delete it:

  1. Open Pocket Playland and tap the settings button (the gear, top right).
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete my online profile, then tap Delete my online profile again to confirm.

Your player tag, nickname, friends list (you are also removed from your friends' lists), friend requests, invites, challenges you created, your challenge and Cup scores and your anonymous online sign-in are deleted from our server straight away. Short-lived match data (game moves) is deleted automatically when a match ends. You need an internet connection for this step.

Delete the game data on your phone (instant)

Your scores, coins, levels and settings are stored only on your phone. We do not keep a copy. To delete them:

  1. Open Pocket Playland and tap the menu button (top left).
  2. Tap Reset all scores and coins, then tap it again to confirm.

Or uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's Settings → Apps → Pocket Playland → Storage → Clear storage. Either way, everything is deleted straight away.

Ask us to delete data (by email)

  1. Email sleepysleve@gmail.com with the subject "Pocket Playland data deletion".
  2. Tell us what you would like deleted. If you want your online profile deleted and can no longer open the app, include your player tag (7 letters and numbers, shown in the app under Profile → Friends) if you know it. If you signed in to Google Play Games, include your Play Games gamer name.
  3. We reply within 30 days and confirm what was deleted.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Online profile (player tag, nickname, avatar, level, friends, requests, invites, challenges, anonymous sign-in): held on our server only while you use online play, deleted in full as described above. We keep no copy after deletion.
  • Game progress and settings: stored only on your device, deleted as described above. Deleting your online profile does not delete them.
  • Google Play Games scores (only if you signed in): these are held by Google. You can delete your Play Games profile and its data at any time in the Google Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, or at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.
  • Advertising data collected by Google AdMob (advertising ID, approximate location, ad interactions): held by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

See also our Privacy Policy.
