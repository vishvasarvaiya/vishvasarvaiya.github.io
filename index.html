Pocket Playland

A big box of cute mini-games for Android that all work without Wi-Fi.

  • Puzzles like sorting, merging, blocks, jigsaws and spot the difference
  • Board and card games like chess, ludo, solitaire and hearts, vs the computer or a friend
  • Arcade and retro games like racing, motocross, cricket, football, boxing and platformers
