Questions

Good to know

Do I need the internet to play?

No. Every game works offline: on a train, on a flight, or when your data runs out.

Is Pocket Playland free?

Yes. It's free with optional ads. You can buy Remove Ads, coin packs, the Pass Gold or VIP, but you never need to.

Will it run on my phone?

It's a small download made for Android, and it runs smoothly on low-end and high-end phones, with 60 and 120 FPS support.

Are more games coming?

Yes. New games and updates are added regularly, and they arrive in the same app.

What happens to my data?

Your progress is saved on your phone. Read the privacy policy for details, or see how to delete your data.
