Pocket Playland Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Pocket Playland ("the app", "we", "us") is a collection of offline mini-games for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be short and plain.

The short version

  • You do not need an account to play, and we never ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • Your scores, coins, levels and settings are saved only on your phone. We do not have a server and we never receive them.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, your high scores are sent to Google for the leaderboards.

Information saved on your device

To remember your progress, the app stores game data in your phone's local storage: scores and best times, levels reached, coins, hints and boosters, favourite games, and settings such as volume, music and vibration. This data stays on your device. It is deleted if you use "Reset all scores and coins" in Settings, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the menu, a full-screen ad between some games, and optional reward ads you can choose to watch for coins or hints. To serve, limit, measure and protect these ads, Google's software in the app may collect:

DataWhy
Advertising ID (a resettable ID set by Android)Showing ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, and measuring ad performance
IP address and approximate location derived from itShowing ads suitable for your region and preventing fraud
Device and app information (model, Android version, language, app version)Making ads display correctly
Ad interactions (ads viewed and tapped) and crash information from the ad softwareMeasuring ads, preventing fraud, fixing problems

This information is collected and handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy → Privacy choices.
  • On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (the exact menu depends on your phone).

Google Play Games (optional)

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, the app sends your scores in supported games to Google so they can appear on leaderboards. Your Play Games profile and gamer name are managed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. Playing without signing in works exactly the same.

Internet access

All games work without the internet. The app uses an internet connection only for ads and, if you sign in, Google Play Games.

Children

Pocket Playland is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone other than the Google services described above.

Security and retention

Game data is kept on your device until you reset it or uninstall the app. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
